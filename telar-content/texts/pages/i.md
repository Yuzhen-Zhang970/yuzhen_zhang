title: "Introduction"
---

\[YELLOW\] Across this Telar exhibition, I examine love not as one single emotion, but as a repeated pattern of care, attention, and connection in everyday campus life. \[/YELLOW\] At UCSB, love appears in many different forms: students studying quietly in the library, friends sitting together at an outdoor table, classmates sharing food in a playful moment, and a couple celebrating graduation through physical closeness and trust. These scenes may look ordinary or casual, but they reveal how emotional support and belonging are created through small actions.

 

\[GREEN\] This exhibition suggests that love at UCSB often appears through presence rather than performance. \[/GREEN\] During my field observations, I noticed that people rarely needed to say love directly for it to become visible. Instead, love appeared through staying near someone, sharing time, laughing together, supporting another person's body, and celebrating a shared milestone. \[YELLOW\] Together, these images show that love is not only private or romantic; it is also shaped by public campus spaces, everyday routines, and moments of mutual care. \[/YELLOW\] My visual narrative argues that love at UCSB is built through small acts of attention that help students feel supported, connected, and less alone.
